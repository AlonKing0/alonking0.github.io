3. **Download the File**:
   Click on the asset for your OS to start the download. The file will usually end in `.exe`, `.tar.gz`, or `.zip`, depending on your system.

4. **Locate the Downloaded File**:
   Once the download completes, find it in your downloads folder or the location where your browser saves files.

5. **Unzip the File (if needed)**:
   If your download is a `.zip` or `.tar.gz` file, unzip it. Right-click on the file and choose "Extract Here" or "Extract All."
